# Eos keys on a PC keyboard: a At, t Thru, f Full, r Record, q Cue, i Time,
# o Out, ; Patch, F1 Live, Alt+A Address, Control+G Go To Cue, Space Go.
# Letters stay lower case, since Shift is Eos's own Shift.
keys {F1}
keys +{BACKSPACE}
wait 2
shot live
# Patch channels 1 to 3 to addresses 1 to 3 of universe 1
keys ;
wait 3
keys 1t3%a1{ENTER}
wait 3
shot patched
keys {F1}
wait 3
# Cue 1 brings channel 1 to 70 in 3 seconds, cue 2 channel 2 to 80 and 3
# to full in 5
keys 1a70{ENTER}
wait 2
keys rq1i3{ENTER}
wait 3
keys 2a80{ENTER}
wait 1
keys 3af{ENTER}
wait 2
keys rq2i5{ENTER}
wait 3
shot recorded
keys ^go{ENTER}
wait 8
shot out
keys ^g1{ENTER}
wait 8
shot cue-1
go
wait 10
shot cue-2
